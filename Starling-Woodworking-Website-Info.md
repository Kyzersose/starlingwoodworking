# About
Our family business was founded to meet the need for durable, high-quality furniture, a value that still guides our work today. Drawing on experience with a master craftsman, we know the importance of lasting, beautiful pieces, which is why we use sturdy hardwoods like poplar, oak, walnut, maple, and cherry. We oversee every detail, crafting our own drawer-slides and hinges to uphold our standards. Our commitment to quality has built both reliable furniture and strong relationships with customers who appreciate superior workmanship.
We offer furniture for bedrooms, living rooms, dens, as well as dining room accessories like buffets and display cabinets. At our Angel’s Antiques location in Opelika, Alabama, we offer three main styles: Farmhouse, Classic, and Craftsman. Each features quality joinery and distinctive elements, designed to suit a wide range of budgets.  
We also undertake custom projects, including mudroom storage benches, bespoke storage sectionals, and tailored sectional entertainment centers.  
# Our Services:
Bedroom Furniture: Choose from Farmhouse, Classic, or Craftsman styles. Add a single piece or furnish the entire room. 
Living Rooms and Dens: Coffee tables, end tables, entertainment consoles, sectionals, desks, and bookcases available. 
Kitchens and Dining Rooms: Custom islands, coffee bars, buffets, and China cabinets. 
Custom Home Decor: Accessories including jewelry boxes, candlesticks, and personalized wall art and Christmas ornaments.
