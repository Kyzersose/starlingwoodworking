Our Story

Quality Is the
Only Standard

Our family business was founded to meet the need for durable, high-quality furniture — a value that still guides every piece we make. Drawing on experience with a master craftsman, we know the importance of lasting, beautiful work.

That's why we use sturdy hardwoods like poplar, oak, walnut, maple, and cherry — and why we oversee every detail, crafting our own drawer slides and hinges to uphold our standards. Our commitment has built both reliable furniture and strong relationships with customers who appreciate superior workmanship.
