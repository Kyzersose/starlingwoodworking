About Us

At Starling Woodworking, we believe that every piece of wood tells a story.

With years of experience and an unwavering commitment to quality, we transform raw materials into timeless pieces that enhance your living spaces. Our dedication to craftsmanship ensures that each project receives the attention to detail it deserves.

From custom furniture to intricate woodwork, we bring your vision to life with precision and passion.

Our Services

Custom Furniture

Bespoke pieces designed and crafted to perfectly fit your space and style. From dining tables to bedroom sets.

Cabinetry

Expert kitchen and bathroom cabinetry, built-ins, and storage solutions that maximize functionality.

Millwork

Fine architectural woodwork including crown molding, wainscoting, and custom trim details.

Restoration

Breathe new life into cherished pieces with our expert furniture restoration and refinishing services.
